            TECHNICAL FAQ
            
Key Answers for Buyers & Engineering Teams

          

        

        
Which drawing formats do you accept for quoting?
We run AutoCAD (CAD) and GibbsCAM (CAM), allowing DWG and DXF drawings emailed to info@precis-meca.fr to be imported directly into programming alongside dimensioned PDF prints.


What are your maximum turning and milling envelopes?
We turn parts from Ø 8 up to Ø 1,600 mm (Gurutzpe CNC up to Ø 1,400 × 3,000 mm; Cazeneuve HB750 up to 4,600 mm length) and mill up to 2,500 × 1,000 × 1,000 mm on our AWEA 4-axis center.


Do you provide inspection reports and material certificates?
Yes. Every machined part is checked in our metrology room (Mitutoyo Crysta-Apex C CMM, LH-600, TESA Unimaster) and shipped with an inspection report. Material certificates are available upon request.
